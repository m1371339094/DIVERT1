    The Context & Gap: Why existing protective tools aren't enough

    sms_failed Silence in Grey Areas

    Standard ad blockers and antivirus tools only detect known malicious URLs and malware binaries. They are completely blind to relational coercion, social engineering, guilt trips, and urgent peer pressure.

    notifications_active Alarm Fatigue & Shame

    Traditional warning popups trigger overwhelming alarm bells and judgment, leaving users feeling ashamed or anxious. DIVERT1 replaces panic with gentle, neuro-inclusive, low-sensory explanations.

    Who It Is Built For

    Neurodivergent Young Adults

    Individuals on the autism spectrum or with ADHD navigating subtle nuance, sarcasm, and disguised malicious intent.

    New to Adult Digital Risks

    First-time bank account holders, college students, and remote freelancers making independent financial decisions.

    Support Network Often Absent

    A 24/7 non-judgmental companion when parents, mentors, or counselors are not immediately available to review a chat.

    1. Main Chatbot Agent (watsonx Orchestrate)

    Manages active multi-turn context, parses uploaded screenshots and messages, and provides conversational pacing.

    shield
    2. Safety Agent (Deterministic Rule Engine)

    Evaluates coercive patterns, financial pressure, urgency spikes, and gift-card flags against calibrated risk indices.

    sentiment_satisfied

    Synthesizes actionable, one-tap reply scripts, boundary-setting advice, and sensory de-escalation guidance.

    Key Differentiators

    Whole-Conversation Analysis

    Looks across conversational turns rather than isolating individual words to catch gradual grooming tactics.

    Explains the Why

    Doesn't just say 'DANGER' — explains exactly what psychological levers are being used and why they work.

    Respects Friendships

    Offers neutral boundary scripts that allow preserving genuine relationships while firmly declining risky favors.

    Avoids Over-Warning

    Calibrated thresholds prevent false alarms that cause users to abandon protective tools altogether.

    policy Responsible Design & MVP Scope

    DIVERT1 is built in strict adherence to neuro-inclusive human-AI interaction guidelines. Conversations are never monetized, trained into public datasets, or used to build commercial profiles. All advice is educational and supportive, empowering individual agency without patronizing surveillance.
