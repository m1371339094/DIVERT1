policy Responsible Design Pledge

Every contributor to DIVERT1 agrees to build in strict adherence to neuro-inclusive human–AI interaction guidelines. Conversations are never monetized, trained into public datasets, or used to build commercial profiles. All advice is educational and supportive, empowering individual agency without patronizing surveillance.
