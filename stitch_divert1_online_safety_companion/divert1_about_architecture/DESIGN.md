## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a calm, supportive, and predictable sanctuary for neurodivergent young adults navigating online safety. The emotional intent is to de-escalate anxiety, eliminate cognitive overload, and establish unconditional psychological safety. Rather than adopting an alarmist, high-friction, or overtly clinical security aesthetic, the visual atmosphere balances warm, reassuring editorial intimacy with functional clarity.

The style merges **warm editorial humanism** with **gentle tactile minimalism**:
- **Cognitive Ease & Low Sensory Friction:** Stark stark-white interfaces and high-saturation neon warning lights trigger sensory fatigue. Instead, warm parchment backgrounds, deeply grounded espresso typography, and earthy terracotta affordances evoke an inviting physical reading notebook or calm offline study room.
- **Predictable & Explicit Visual Structure:** Information relies on clear visual nesting, deliberate hierarchy, generous touch targets, and unambiguous micro-interactions.
- **Dignified Agency:** Designed to treat users as capable young adults seeking clarity rather than children needing condescension. Reassuring editorial serifs grant authority and dignity, while crisp neutral body text delivers immediate legibility.

## Colors

The palette grounds users in natural, sun-warmed earth tones designed to regulate nervous system arousal while strictly maintaining WCAG AAA/AA readability standards:

- **Primary (`#c0622f` Terracotta):** Used for focal action points, primary buttons, confirmed progression states, and active navigational highlights.
- **Secondary (`#5a9e6e` Calm Forest Green):** Represents safety, resolved issues, verified links, and reassuring affirmations without clinical starkness.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706` Warm Amber):** Reserved for contextual warnings, cautionary prompts, and items requiring mindfulness. It advises care without causing panic.
- **Neutral Deep / Text Primary (`#2e1f0f` Dark Espresso):** High-contrast, soft-edged typography base providing deep legibility against light cream surfaces without the aggressive contrast spike of pure `#000000`.
- **Deep Neutral / Sidebar Anchor (`#3b2210` Roasted Coffee):** Structural containment for high-level navigation, fostering grounding and persistent spatial orientation.
- **Secondary Text (`#7a5c40` Warm Brown) & Muted Text (`#b08866` Caramel):** Metadata, supportive hints, and tertiary annotations.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Page Canvas: `#fdf6ee` (Warm parchment)
  - Resting Card Surface: `#fff9f2` (Warm cream)
  - Surface Accent / Interactive Soft: `#fdefd8` (Soft peach/butter)
  - Structural Hairlines & Dividers: `#e8d5bc` (Warm tan)
  - Accent Soft Light: `#f5c8a8` (Blush/warm clay for badges and highlight fills)

## Typography

The typographical pairing reinforces reassurance and focus. 

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** An authoritative, warm, and literary serif that commands attention gently. It imparts a quiet, reflective quality suitable for guidance, introspective prompts, and reassuring declarations.
- **Body & Interface (`Inter`):** Selected for its neutral clarity, x-height, and legibility at all scales. It ensures that technical details, safety steps, and critical decisions can be scanned without cognitive fatigue or visual ambiguity.
- **Leading & Measure:** Running body text is constrained to an optimal 60–75 characters per line with generous line height (`1.5` to `1.6`) to prevent visual crowding or skipping lines during reading.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8px spatial grid, prioritizing generous negative space to reduce sensory stress. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column fluid grid bounded by a max-width container of `1200px` to maintain comfortable eye movement. The persistent navigation rail or sidebar occupies 3 columns or a dedicated 280px docked zone.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** An 8-column fluid grid with `2rem` outer page margins. Auxiliary support information nests beneath core interaction workflows.
- **Mobile (<640px):** A single 4-column flow with `1rem` margin safeguards. Complex multi-step actions convert into single-focus vertical sequences, preventing cognitive competition.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** All interactive targets must be separated by at least `space-sm` (8px) to eliminate accidental activations. Card interiors leverage `space-md` or `space-lg` to create a dedicated buffer zone around copy.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid visual disorientation and artificiality, this design system rejects heavy directional drop-shadows and stark floating elevations. Depth is expressed through **tactile surface layering** and **warm ambient tinting**:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Grounded `#fdf6ee` page backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Raised `#fff9f2` surface bounded by a crisp 1px `#e8d5bc` outline and a soft, diffused ambient glow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(46, 31, 15, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Containers):** Subtle physical lift with surface elevation `#fffdfa`, reinforced border `#d9c2a3`, and a slightly deeper grounding shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(46, 31, 15, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Overlays & Prompts):** Tinted backdrop scrim (`rgba(46, 31, 15, 0.35)`) overlaid with `#fff9f2` surfaces framed by `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(46, 31, 15, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses deliberate, rounded geometry (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for containers) to project warmth, softness, and physical approachability. Sharp 90-degree corners are strictly avoided on surface containers to minimize unconscious perceptual tension.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Cards, Alert Containers, Content Blocks:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Floating Modals & Guidance Panels:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Status Indicators & Micro-Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to emphasize non-threatening touchability.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button:** Background `#c0622f`, text `#ffffff`, border radius `0.5rem`, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, minimum height `48px`. Focus states show a 3px ring in `#f5c8a8` with a 2px offset. Hover shifts to `#ad5526`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#fdefd8`, text `#2e1f0f`, 1px solid border in `#e8d5bc`. Hover transitions to `#f7e3c3`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#7a5c40`, hover text `#2e1f0f`, with subtle `#fdefd8` surface illumination on hover.

### Cards & Guidance Modules
- Standard containers use `#fff9f2` fill, 1px `#e8d5bc` border, `1rem` corner rounding, and `1.5rem` internal padding.
- Interactive cards provide an unambiguous hover feedback: border darkens to `#d9c2a3` and elevation rises to Level 2.
- **Safety Status Cards:** Use dedicated accent bars on the left edge (`4px` solid `#5a9e6e` for Safe, `#d97706` for Attention) combined with matching pastel tint fills (`#5a9e6e` at 8% opacity).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e8d5bc`, text `#2e1f0f`, placeholder `#b08866`. Height is fixed at `48px` to ensure touch comfort. Focus state produces a solid `#c0622f` border and a soft terracotta focus aura.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `22px` diameter, `#e8d5bc` border, `#ffffff` surface. Checked state fills `#c0622f` with a pure white glyph.

### Status Chips & Pills
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, font size `13px` (`label-md`).
- **Safe:** Surface `rgba(90, 158, 110, 0.12)`, text `#3a6948`, border 1px solid `rgba(90, 158, 110, 0.3)`.
- **Warning:** Surface `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`, text `#924e04`, border 1px solid `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`.
- **Neutral Tag:** Surface `#fdefd8`, text `#7a5c40`, border 1px solid `#e8d5bc`.

### Lists & Scannable Trees
- Row items are separated by subtle hairlines (`#e8d5bc`) or nested within soft `#fff9f2` discrete cards. Iconography or numbered badges guide step-by-step resolution without dense visual clustering.
