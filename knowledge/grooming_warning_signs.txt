# Grooming Warning Signs

This document is part of the Online Safety Companion knowledge base.
It is written at a pattern level to support risk assessment — not as a script.

---

## What Grooming Is

Grooming is a process. A person builds trust with a young person (and sometimes
their family) over time, then uses that trust to manipulate or exploit them.
It can happen online or in person. It is deliberate and gradual.

---

## Early Stage Signals

These signals appear at the start of a relationship and may seem harmless alone.
Their significance increases when several appear together.

| Signal | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Excessive attention | Constant messages, "good morning" and "good night" every day, always available |
| Rapid intimacy | Saying "I feel like I've known you forever" after just days or hours |
| Special status | "You're so mature for your age", "you're not like other people your age" |
| Gift-giving | Sending game credits, vouchers, or small gifts early in the relationship |
| Shared secrets | "Let's keep this between us", "I don't tell just anyone this" |

---

## Escalation Signals

These appear once some trust has been established.

| Signal | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Platform migration | "Add me on Telegram / Snapchat / WhatsApp — it's more private" |
| Secrecy from family | "Your parents wouldn't understand", "don't tell your mum about this" |
| Isolation | Encouraging the young person to spend less time with friends or family |
| Testing boundaries | Gradually introducing sexual topics or jokes to gauge reaction |
| Emotional dependency | "You're the only person who gets me", "I don't know what I'd do without you" |

---

## High-Risk Signals

These require immediate escalation.

| Signal | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Photo/video requests | Requesting intimate images; may start with "just a normal photo" |
| Sextortion leverage | "I have your photos — pay me or I'll send them to everyone" |
| In-person meeting | Asking to meet alone, offering to pick them up, suggesting a secret location |
| Threats | Threatening to expose content, harm the person, or contact their family |
| Money requests | Asking for gift cards, bank transfers, or cryptocurrency |

---

## Why It Is Hard to Recognize

- Grooming can feel like a genuine friendship or romantic relationship.
- The groomer often provides emotional support the young person values.
- Neurodivergent young people may have fewer social comparison points and may
  find it harder to recognize when a relationship pattern is unusual.
- The gradual escalation means each individual step can seem small.

---

## Key Principle

The presence of one or two signals does not confirm grooming. Multiple signals
together — especially secrecy, platform migration, and photo or money requests —
significantly increase risk.
